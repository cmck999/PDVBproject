PDVoice Buddy
A light that stays on while your voice is carrying


WHAT IT DOES
------------

Parkinson's makes voices quieter without the person noticing. You feel like
you're speaking normally; the room hears a whisper.

PDVoice Buddy listens to the room and shows you one thing: a glowing dot.
Bright means your voice is carrying. Dim means it's fading, and it's time to
push a little.

That's the whole app. No exercises, no scores, nothing to read while you're
mid-sentence.

It works out how loud the room is and asks you to rise above it. That means it
adjusts itself — a quiet kitchen and a busy café both work, without you
changing anything.


STARTING IT
-----------

Tap the PDVoice Buddy icon — two people at a table.

The first time, it asks to use the microphone. Say yes.

Nothing is ever recorded. It measures how loud the room is, a dozen times a
second, keeps that one number, and throws the sound away. There is no
recording to find, because none is ever made.


THE FIRST FEW SECONDS
---------------------

When it starts, it says "Listening to the room" for a second or two. That's
it learning what your room sounds like with nobody talking.

If you move to a different room, or the room gets much noisier, close it and
open it again so it can relearn.


USING IT
--------

Just talk. Watch the dot out of the corner of your eye.

  Bright and glowing ......... They can hear you. Keep going.
  Dim and small .............. You're fading. Speak up.
  Growing and shrinking ...... It follows every word, so you can see
                               yourself getting louder before you reach
                               the target.

Don't stare at it. The dot is meant for the corner of your eye, while your
attention stays on the person you're talking to.


THE BUTTONS
-----------

  Pause (two bars) ..... Stops it listening. Press it for private calls, or
                         when you're watching television. Press again — it
                         becomes a triangle — to start listening again.

  Gear ................. Settings.

  You can also tap anywhere on the screen to open settings.


SETTINGS
--------

Four things, and you'll probably only ever change the first.

HOW LOUD YOU NEED TO BE
    Five choices, from Gentle to Strong.

    Pick one you can already manage. If the dot is dark most of the time,
    the setting is too high — go down a step. You want a dot that's lit more
    often than not.

    If you're working with a speech therapist, ask them where to set it, and
    raise it a step every week or two rather than all at once.

COLOUR
    Mint, amber, sky or white. Whichever you notice most easily.

SIZE
    Full screen — a large dot in the middle. Easiest to see across a room.

    Small pill — a narrow bar that floats on top of whatever else you're
    doing, so you can use the tablet normally. Drag it wherever you like; it
    remembers. It has its own pause, gear and ✕ buttons, and the ✕ closes
    the app.

    The first time you choose Small pill, Android will send you to a settings
    screen to allow it. Allow it, then come back — the pill will be running.

IN THE CAR
    A tablet cannot tell whether you're driving or in the passenger seat, so
    it has to ask.

    Not in a car ...... normal.
    I'm a passenger ... the dot stays on. Nobody is steering.
    I'm driving ....... the screen goes dark and you get a short buzz when
                        your voice drops. Nothing to look at.

    IMPORTANT: this resets every time you open the app. If you're going to
    drive, set it to "I'm driving" each time. A moving light in a driver's
    line of sight is dangerous and against the law in many places.


LEAVING IT RUNNING
------------------

While it's listening there's a notice in the top bar of the tablet. That's
Android's rule, not a choice — any app using the microphone has to show one.
You can tap it to come back to PDVoice Buddy.

To stop it completely: the ✕ on the pill, or just close the app.


IF SOMETHING'S WRONG
--------------------

The dot never lights up, even when I speak up
    The setting is probably too high. Open settings and go down a step or
    two — try Easy or Gentle.

The dot is always lit, even when I whisper
    Something in the room is noisy — a television, a fan, a dishwasher. Turn
    it off, then close and reopen the app so it relearns the room.

It says "Listening to the room" and stays that way
    Give it two seconds. If it doesn't change, close and reopen it.

The dot doesn't react at all
    Check the microphone is allowed: tablet Settings > Apps > PDVoice Buddy >
    Permissions > Microphone.

I chose Small pill and nothing happened
    Android needs permission for a floating pill. Tap Small pill again and
    it'll take you to the right screen.

I can't find the pill
    It may have been dragged off the edge. Open the app from its icon and
    switch to Full screen, then back to Small pill.


ONE HONEST NOTE
---------------

This is a homemade tool, not a medical device.

It's good at telling louder from quieter in one place. It is not a
measurement, and it should not be used to make any decision about treatment.

Use it as a reminder. Set the level with your speech-language pathologist
rather than guessing, especially if you're already doing speech therapy.

If the dot helps you stay audible, that's the whole point. If it turns into
one more thing nagging at you, close it and say so — it can be changed.
