PDVOICE BUDDY — INSTALLING ON WINDOWS
User-facing paragraph. Copy this wherever you're handing out the app.


SHORT — for a download page or an email
=======================================

PDVoice Buddy is a single file, so there's no real installation. Download
PDVoice Buddy.exe, put it somewhere you'll find it again — your Desktop is
fine — and double-click it. The first time, Windows will probably show a blue
box saying "Windows protected your PC"; that's expected, and it only means the
app doesn't come from a big software company. Click "More info", then "Run
anyway", and it will start. Windows may then ask whether the app can use your
microphone: say yes, or the light won't work. A small pill appears in the
corner of your screen, and after a second or two it's listening. Nothing else
to set up.

VERY SHORT — one or two lines
=============================

Download PDVoice Buddy.exe and double-click it. If Windows shows a blue
"Windows protected your PC" box, click "More info" then "Run anyway" — that's
normal for a small app like this. Allow the microphone when asked, and you're
running.


A NOTE FOR WHOEVER PUBLISHES THIS
=================================

Keep the blue-warning sentence, and keep it early. It is the single most
common reason someone deletes an unsigned app and assumes it's broken or
unsafe. Naming it before they see it turns alarm into a shrug.

Keeping "say yes, or the light won't work" matters for the same reason: a
denied microphone leaves the app looking dead, with nothing on screen
explaining why.
